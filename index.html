QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Is Gunventory a subscription?

No. The current Gunventory Premium offering is a one-time purchase.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium removes ads and unlocks PC Companion access. Individual purchases are also available.

Can I keep manuals with my firearm records?

Yes. Gunventory supports attaching owner's manuals and other documents to firearm records.

Does Gunventory replace manufacturer maintenance instructions?

No. Gunventory is a recordkeeping application. Follow manufacturer documentation for model-specific procedures.

How does the PC Companion connect?

The current companion workflow synchronizes with the Android app over a compatible local Wi‑Fi network.
